# Asterion Foundry — Command & Rank Structure

*Archived verbatim from `Asterion Foundry — Command & Rank Structure.md`
(Perplexity design chat, saved by Ryan). This is the source design draft
referenced throughout `lore/canon.md`, `lore/glossary.md`, and
`lore/gaps.md` for the Council/Branch structure — see those files for
what's actually confirmed as canon vs. what's still design-draft only.*

**Status (per the source doc itself):** Design draft, not yet
implemented in code or committed to the repo.
**Fits with:** `DESIGN_SPINE.md` — Earth as political capital, Mars as
first forge world, the Asterion Collegium as Mars's research
institution, and the Directorate as humanity's supreme governing body.

**v4 — adds the bureaucracy, the secret police, and the ones who answer
to no one.** Styled after Warhammer 40K's Administratum and Inquisition,
plus Star Wars's ISB, again using original names with the parallel
called out in parentheses.

**What's in this version:** a 10-seat Council, eight branches with full
territorial ladders (A–H), two new bureaucratic/security branches (I–J),
and — deliberately breaking the pattern — one organization that sits
outside the whole org chart on purpose (K), plus a short list of things
that exist *outside* the Directorate entirely.

---

## The scaling rule (unchanged)

1. **Territory** scales through **World/Fleet → Sector → Dominion →
   (optional) Reach**.
2. **The Council** only grows when a genuinely new institution of state
   is founded. It's now at ten seats. One institution in this version —
   the Vigil — deliberately does *not* get a seat, because its entire
   premise is operating outside the normal chain of command.

## Tier 0 — Supreme Command

**Grand Director** *(the player)* — including the optional path to
becoming **the Undying Flame** as the Ember Faith's living/divine center
*(NOTE: superseded — see the religion-removal retcon; the Ember Faith
and Undying Flame framing no longer apply, kept here only as historical
record of this draft's original text)*.

### The Director's Own *(40K parallel: the Adeptus Custodes)*

A small elite personal guard, loyal to the Director as a person, not to
any branch or faction. Deliberately outside Navy/Army politics. No
echelon structure like the other branches — sized to stay with the
Director wherever they physically are.

---

## Tier 1 — The Directorate Council

Ten seats.

| Seat | Domain | Reports to |
|---|---|---|
| **First Minister** | Civil government, population, food & life support, policy | Grand Director |
| **Forge-Marshal** | All forge worlds' heavy industry, refining, fabrication, shipbuilding | Grand Director |
| **High Savant** | The Asterion Collegium and every chapter it grows | Grand Director |
| **Grand Admiral** | All military fleets, escorts, naval doctrine | Grand Director |
| **Quartermaster-General** | Freight, convoys, supply routes, logistics doctrine | Grand Director |
| **Colonial Warden** | Survey, colonization, colony specialization | Grand Director |
| **Marshal-General** | The Legions — all standing ground forces | Grand Director |
| **The First Flamekeeper** *(superseded — see retcon)* | The Ember Faith, its priesthood, and the Sisters of the Ember | Grand Director |
| **Chancellor-General** *(40K parallel: the Administratum)* | The Chancellery — tithes, census, records, cross-branch reporting | Grand Director |
| **Provost-General** *(40K parallel: the Adeptus Arbites / ISB)* | The Bureau — counter-espionage, loyalty oversight, internal security | Grand Director |

Navy, Army, and the Sisters of the Ember remain three separate armed
forces, none outranking the others. The Bureau adds a fourth kind of
power center — not armed the same way, but capable of investigating
*any* of the other nine seats.

## Branch I — The Chancellery *(40K parallel: the Administratum)*

The bureaucracy that keeps every other branch honest and countable.
Runs the **tithe** — a periodic resource quota every world owes the
Directorate, distinct from voluntary trade — plus census, records, and
standardized reporting across all nine other branches.

| Rank | Echelon | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Chancellor-General | Council | The Chancellery empire-wide |
| Dominion Chancellor | Dominion | Records and tithes in one Dominion |
| Sector Chancellor | Sector | Records and tithes in one Sector |
| World Registrar | World | Census/tithe office for one world |
| Tithe Clerk | World | Collects and processes resource quotas |
| Scrivener | World | Record-keeper |
| Adept-Scrivener | World | Trainee |

**Tithe convoys** are a natural extension of the CSV Meridian's supply-
run pattern — a freighter hauling tithe goods to a Chancellery depot is
mechanically identical to one hauling support supplies to Mars.

## Branch J — The Bureau *(40K parallel: the Adeptus Arbites / ISB)*

Secular internal security: counter-espionage, loyalty surveillance of
officials and officers, suppressing dissent, watching for external
threats. Methodical and bureaucratic where the Vigil is freewheeling and
unaccountable — the two bodies have overlapping jurisdiction over
"loyalty" and are meant to distrust each other.

| Rank | Echelon | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Provost-General | Council | The Bureau empire-wide |
| Dominion Provost | Dominion | Internal security in one Dominion |
| Sector Provost | Sector | Internal security in one Sector |
| Station Chief | World | The Bureau's presence on one world |
| Case Officer | World | Manages a portfolio of investigations |
| Field Agent | World | Surveillance and investigation |
| Informant | Unranked | Recruited civilian assets |

## Branch K — The Vigil *(40K parallel: the Inquisition — deliberately outside the org chart)*

Everything above follows World → Sector → Dominion → Council seat. The
Vigil doesn't, on purpose. It exists to investigate corruption within
the Directorate itself, xenos contact, rogue technology, and anything
else that threatens the Grand Director's rule at a level the other nine
branches aren't positioned to catch — including each other. *(Its
original mandate also covered "heresy against the Faith" — superseded by
the religion-removal retcon; see `religion-removal-retcon.md` for the
reworked mandate around cult activity/religious revivalism instead.)*

- **First Seekers** — a small circle of the most senior, trusted
  Seekers. Sometimes advising the Grand Director directly and privately.
- **Seeker** — an individual agent with sweeping, near-unchecked
  authority. Can commandeer resources and override officers of almost
  any rank in pursuit of a mandate, answering only to the Director.
- **Seeker's Hand** — an apprentice learning under a Seeker.
- **Agents** — specialists, soldiers, and informants a Seeker recruits
  ad hoc for a specific case, with no permanent rank or unit.

**A hard question worth sitting on (source doc's own note):** whose
mandate does the Vigil actually answer to — the person currently on the
throne, or "the office of the Grand Director" as an institution?

**The Silent Blades** — mentioned, not detailed on purpose. A tiny,
extremely secretive deniable-operations cadre the Vigil (or the Director
personally) can call on for assassination or sabotage far behind enemy
lines.

## Outside the Directorate entirely

- **Chartered Trading Houses** *(40K parallel: Rogue Traders)* —
  independent merchant houses licensed by the Directorate to explore and
  trade autonomously, especially past the Dominion frontier. Pay tithes
  to the Chancellery but otherwise self-govern.
- **The Wayfarers' Guild** — charting star-lanes, running relay
  stations, keeping communication fast across huge distances. Could sit
  under Logistics (Branch F) or the Collegium (Branch C) — parent
  branch still undecided.

## Gameplay hooks (source doc's own notes, for later, not now)

- **Four kinds of power, not three.** Navy, Army, and the Sisters
  answer to the Director through three Council seats; the Bureau can
  investigate all of them; the Vigil can override any of them and
  answers to no seat at all.
- **The tithe as a pressure valve.** A world that can't meet its tithe
  is a Chancellery problem before it's a rebellion.
- **Loyalty as a trackable, contested resource.** The Bureau tracks it
  one way (compliance, surveillance), the Vigil another (in the original
  draft: faith/purity — superseded by human self-determination/autonomy
  per the retcon).
