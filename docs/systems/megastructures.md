# Megastructures and Mobile Civilization — Future Design

**Status:** Long-term design only. These systems should be constructed from mechanics already tested at smaller scale.

## Design principle

A hive world or Dyson project must not be a one-click purchase. It should be the final form of the logistics, construction, industry, research, and defense systems the player already understands.

## Hive cities

A hive city is a massive layered urban-industrial complex. It concentrates population, manufacturing, research, recruitment, and administration.

### Needs

- Continuous food and water
- Enormous power
- Housing and transit infrastructure
- Maintenance goods and spare parts
- Security and emergency services
- Freight capacity and orbital ports

### Payoff

- Large workforce
- High industrial and research output
- Major recruitment and administrative capacity

## Hive worlds

A hive world is a planet-scale network of dense industrial cities and infrastructure.

### Project sequence

```text
Develop colony
→ create multiple hive cities
→ expand planetary power, transport, and life support
→ integrate industrial chains and orbital traffic
→ meet population, throughput, and technology thresholds
→ begin world conversion stages
```

### Risk

The world becomes extremely productive but dependent on vast continuous flows. A blockade, famine, infrastructure collapse, or invasion creates a civilization-scale crisis.

## Nomadic fleets

A nomadic fleet is a mobile strategic community or industrial expedition: a colony with engines.

### First model

Track only:

- Population capacity
- Fuel reserve
- Cargo capacity
- Habitat integrity
- Repair capacity
- Industrial capacity
- Current mission
- Escort strength

### Possible roles

- First-colony expedition
- Mobile forge fleet
- Frontier resupply group
- Evacuation fleet
- Military campaign train
- Independent future faction

## Dyson swarm

A Dyson swarm is a modular network of collectors, factories, habitats, and energy infrastructure orbiting a star.

### Construction loop

```text
Survey system
→ secure resources and build extraction
→ establish construction docks and local foundries
→ produce and deliver segments
→ assemble segments over time
→ defend and maintain the network
→ use increasing energy and industrial output
```

Each segment should use the same construction, shipping, upkeep, and defense rules as smaller projects—only at vastly larger scale.

## Dyson sphere / stellar enclosure

Treat a sphere or near-total enclosure as a late completion tier beyond a mature swarm. It is a civilization-defining project requiring extraordinary material throughput, advanced research, time, labor, power-routing systems, and system defense.

### Payoff

- Extreme energy availability
- Exceptional manufacturing and research capability
- Major strategic prestige and vulnerability

## Dependencies

- Multi-system map
- Colonies and world specialization
- Construction queues
- Shipyards and construction fleets
- Resource throughput and storage
- Advanced research
- Strategic defense and fleets
